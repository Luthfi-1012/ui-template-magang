## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-altitude editorial posture tailored for sovereign wealth advisory, multinational holding structures, and state-owned enterprise (BUMN) leadership. The aesthetic fuses **Minimalist Editorial** discipline with **Architectural Bento** modularity—delivering dense analytical clarity alongside high-prestige refinement.

The atmosphere is intellectual, decisive, and grounded. It replaces typical corporate blandness with a warm, curated materiality: tactile linen-grade backdrops juxtaposed against deep organic obsidian modules and punctuated by surgical chartreuse signals. The interface projects permanence, institutional gravity, and progressive forward vision.

## Colors

The palette balances heritage neutrality with high-energy strategic punctuation:

- **Canvas Neutral (`#F9F8F3`)**: A warm, unbleached linen-cream that eliminates digital glare while offering an editorial, paper-like tactile ground.
- **Primary Ink & Obsidian (`#141713`)**: A deep, warm olive-tinted black applied to high-tier card containers, authoritative headlines, and structural anchor blocks.
- **Electric Lime / Chartreuse Accent (`#E2F163`)**: A high-visibility, forward-looking beacon used selectively for primary interactive triggers, active statuses, strategic highlights, and key metric differentials.
- **Muted Olive Slate (`#6D7068`)**: Secondary typography, captions, axis lines, and non-active state indicators.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Bright, crisp white utilized for primary Bento data cells sitting atop the warm linen background.
- **Hairline Border (`#E6E4DC`)**: Subdued structural perimeter line that maintains grid legibility without visual clutter.

## Typography

The single-family typographic architecture relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, deployed with precise optical hierarchies to balance European modernism and contemporary corporate governance.

- **Display & Large Headlines**: Set with aggressive negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.02em`) and tight leading, providing an architectural and monumental editorial tone.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: Shift into semibold and bold weights with open tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`), often rendered uppercase for regulatory tags, sector classifications, and currency designations.
- **Data Densities**: Tabular numerals are enforced across all financial metrics, percentage shifts, and fiscal indices to maintain alignment within Bento panels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column Bento framework calibrated for modular data aggregation:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) outer boundary margins. Max content boundary capped at 1440px for high-density legibility.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Modules reflow into multi-row paired clusters.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Bento modules collapse vertically into full-width cards.

Internal component padding prioritizes generous internal negative space (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) inside structural cards, ensuring key governance figures breathe comfortably without visual friction.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is attained through **chromatic contrast and low-contrast surface architecture** rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Warm off-white (`#F9F8F3`) field representing base administrative context.
- **Level 1 (Bento Surfaces)**:
  - *Luminous Cards*: Solid `#FFFFFF` enclosed by crisp 1px hairline borders (`#E6E4DC`).
  - *Executive Cards*: Deep `#141713` olive-black surfaces providing high-contrast editorial anchors.
- **Shadow Profile**: When required for transient overlays (dropdown menus, contextual executive actions), elevation is rendered via ultra-subtle, warm ambient diffusion: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 23, 19, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 23, 19, 0.02)`.
- **Subtle Layering**: Translucent chartreuse glowing pills overlay dark surfaces with 12% alpha backdrops to signal real-time market or audit alerts.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is defined by two contrasting extremes:

- **Bento Modules & Enclosures**: Precision-molded with an intentional **28px (`1.75rem`)** corner radius, lending an organic, contemporary device aesthetic to macro structural containers.
- **Interactive Controls & Status Badges**: Pure pill geometry (`9999px` border-radius) applied to primary buttons, search filters, state toggles, and metadata chips.
- **Internal Form Fields & Sub-panels**: Mid-tier corner radius of 12px (`0.75rem`) ensures harmonious nesting inside the larger 28px perimeter cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: Chartreuse fill (`#E2F163`) with `#141713` charcoal typography, pill-shaped (`9999px`), semibold `label-md`. Hover state slightly darkens chartreuse saturation with a 1px inner stroke.
- **Secondary Dark**: Deep olive-black fill (`#141713`) with `#FFFFFF` text and 1px `#141713` border.
- **Tertiary Minimal**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E6E4DC` hairline border, `#111210` text, transitioning on hover to subtle linen-cream fill (`#F9F8F3`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`), strictly height-restricted (24px to 28px).
- BUMN Holding / SOE entity tags utilize `#141713` background with `#E2F163` text.
- Operational status indicators use soft neutral backgrounds (`#F0EFEA`) with `#6D7068` text and a 6px status dot.

### Bento Modules & Metric Cards
- Standard white cards feature a 1px border (`#E6E4DC`), 28px border radius, and internal padding of `space-xl` (40px).
- Executive Dark Cards feature `#141713` background, pure white headlines, `#6D7068` muted helper text, and `#E2F163` numeric highlights.
- Metric cells pair oversized tabular values (`headline-xl`) with compact uppercase titles (`label-sm`).

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background: `#FFFFFF` inside canvas, or `#1E231D` inside dark modules.
- Border: 1px hairline (`#E6E4DC`), transitioning to `#141713` on focus with no drop shadow.
- Border radius: 12px for structural cohesion.
- Secondary labels placed exterior-top in `label-sm` tracking.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkboxes: 20px squares with 6px roundedness. Unchecked has 1px `#E6E4DC` border; checked state fills with `#141713` and displays an `#E2F163` check glyph.
- Segmented Switching Bars: Full pill container (`#EFECE4`) containing individual active pills (`#FFFFFF` with micro-shadow) for selecting quarters, portfolios, or enterprise divisions.
